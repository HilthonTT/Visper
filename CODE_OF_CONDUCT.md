# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and welcoming; assume good intent.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour can be reported to the maintainers through
a private [GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/HilthonTT/Visper/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting [@HilthonTT](https://github.com/HilthonTT) directly. All
reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors,
for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
